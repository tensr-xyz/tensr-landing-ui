---
title: Building a plugin
description: Submit a plugin zip for review, price it in pounds, and see earnings once it sells.
---

Open **Plugins**, then **Publish a plugin**.

## What you submit

The form is the manifest (`plugin.toml`) plus a zip. You set the name, version, description, and entry point. Permissions are read from the manifest inside the zip.

The language list is Python, R, JavaScript, and TypeScript. A zip can be submitted for JavaScript or TypeScript today. Python and R are on the form and marked coming soon.

Submit sends the zip for review. The review card shows whether the static scan passed, and whether the plugin is pending, published, or rejected. A plugin that asks for network access is refused. Plugins run with no network access.

## Price and earnings

Choose free, or a monthly subscription priced in pounds. Purchases are taken through Stripe, and the earnings card shows purchases, customers, and this month’s total in pounds once you have sales.
