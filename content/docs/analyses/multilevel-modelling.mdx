## When to use it

Use multilevel modelling when a numeric outcome sits inside groups and you want the intraclass correlation as well as the slopes. The ICC is the share of score variance that sits between groups. A high ICC means people in the same clinic resemble each other.

This procedure fits the same REML mixed model as the [linear mixed model](/docs/analyses/linear-mixed-model). The request uses different field names, and the report adds the ICC and a variance split.

## Assumptions

The outcome and the level-1 predictors are numeric. The level-2 column has at least two groups. This fit uses REML and a random intercept. It does not test residual normality.

## Running it in Tensr

<Steps>
<Step>Multivariate → Mixed Models → Multilevel Modelling (HLM). In chat: “Multilevel model of score on hours, with clinic as level 2.”</Step>
<Step>Outcome is the level-1 score. Level-1 predictors need at least one column. Level-2 grouping variable is the cluster.</Step>
</Steps>

## Reading the output

Same 96 scores as the [linear mixed model](/docs/analyses/linear-mixed-model) page: twelve clinics, eight people each, outcome `score`, predictor `hours`.

| Term | Estimate | Std. Err | p-value |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| const | 50.5168 | 0.97817 | p < .001 |
| hours | 2.01735 | 0.0961672 | p < .001 |

| Level | Variance | % of total |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Between clinic | 9.08003 | 72.06% |
| Within (residual) | 3.52042 | 27.94% |

ICC is 0.721. That is the between-clinic variance divided by between plus residual, from this model that already includes hours. About 72% of the remaining variation sits between clinics. The hours slope matches the linear mixed model on the same rows. The metrics line shows ICC 0.721, 96 observations, and 12 groups.

The charts are “Fitted vs residual” and “Random intercepts by group.” The bar chart shows up to 12 group intercepts.

An ICC below 0.05 would add a sentence that the grouping variable accounts for negligible variance. This run is far above that line, so that sentence is absent.

## Reporting (APA 7)

A multilevel model predicted exam score from study hours with a random intercept for clinic (N = 96, 12 clinics). ICC = .72. Each extra hour was associated with 2.02 points, p < .001.

## Coming from SPSS

The product menu is Multivariate → Mixed Models → Multilevel Modelling (HLM). SPSS fits this with MIXED. The ICC is the ratio of the random-intercept variance to the total variance.
