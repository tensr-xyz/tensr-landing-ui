## When to use it

Use a GLMM when the outcome is yes-or-no, or a count, and the rows sit inside groups. The binomial family reports an odds ratio. The Poisson family is for counts and drops that column.

The fit is a variational Bayes random-intercept model. A binary outcome with an overall effect, and no random intercept you need to interpret, is [GEE](/docs/analyses/gee).

## Assumptions

Binomial needs two outcome values. Poisson needs a non-negative count. The grouping column has at least two clusters. Fixed effects are numeric. This page does not print a convergence warning when the variational fit fails to settle, so read a wild standard error as a reason to rerun on a larger sample.

## Running it in Tensr

<Steps>
<Step>Multivariate → Mixed Models → Generalized Linear Mixed Model (GLMM). In chat: “GLMM of passed on hours, grouped by clinic.”</Step>
<Step>Family starts at Binomial. Poisson is the other choice. The dependent label follows the family: binary, or a count.</Step>
<Step>Grouping variable is the cluster. Fixed effects need at least one numeric predictor.</Step>
</Steps>

## Reading the output

Twelve clinics, fifteen people in each (N = 180). Family is binomial. The outcome is `passed` (95 of 180). The predictor is `hours`.

| Term | Estimate | Std. Err | Odds ratio | p-value |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| const | -0.98837 | 0.157343 | 0.372 | < .001 |
| hours | 0.372139 | 0.0463393 | 1.451 | < .001 |

The hours odds ratio is 1.451, so the odds of passing are larger at higher hours in this set. p < .001. Metrics are Family binomial, 180 observations, and 12 groups. This fit does not print an AIC. The report notes that AIC is not available for this GLMM fit.

The chart is a scatter titled “Fitted vs residual.”

Poisson uses the same request with `family` set to `poisson`. The coefficient table then has Term, Estimate, Std. Err, and p-value, and no odds-ratio column.

## Reporting (APA 7)

A binomial GLMM predicted passing from study hours with a random intercept for clinic (N = 180, 12 clinics). Each extra hour was associated with higher odds of passing, odds ratio = 1.45, p < .001.

## Coming from SPSS

The product menu is Multivariate → Mixed Models → Generalized Linear Mixed Model (GLMM). SPSS fits generalized linear mixed models in GENLINMIXED. This Tensr fit is a variational Bayes random intercept.

## Related

An overall effect for a clustered yes-or-no outcome, without this random intercept, is [GEE](/docs/analyses/gee). One row per person: [logistic regression](/docs/analyses/logistic-regression) or [Poisson regression](/docs/analyses/poisson-regression).
