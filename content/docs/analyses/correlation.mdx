## When to use it

Use a correlation when you want the strength and direction of a relationship, not a prediction equation. For example: do students who study more hours also have higher exam scores, and do more anxious students have lower scores?

Pearson’s r describes a straight-line relationship. Spearman’s ρ and Kendall’s τ are method options on this same page. They rank the scores first, so a curve or a few extreme scores moves them less.

## Assumptions

Pearson’s r assumes a straight line and is pulled around by outliers. Spearman and Kendall do not assume a straight line. Tensr does not test normality or linearity inside this procedure.

## Running it in Tensr

<Steps>
<Step>Analyze → Correlate → Bivariate. In chat: “Correlate hours, anxiety, and score.”</Step>
<Step>Choose the numeric columns. If you leave columns empty, Tensr uses the numeric columns it can find.</Step>
<Step>Method starts at pearson. Tail starts at two_tailed. Significance alpha starts at 0.05. Flag significant starts off.</Step>
</Steps>

## Reading the output

Twenty-four students. `hours` runs from 2 to 8, `anxiety` from 2 to 9, and `score` from 55 to 91. This run left `flag_significant` off.

The coefficient matrix and a separate p-value matrix:

| | hours | anxiety | score |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| hours | 1 | -0.926*** | 0.982*** |
| anxiety | -0.926*** | 1 | -0.967*** |
| score | 0.982*** | -0.967*** | 1 |

| | hours | anxiety | score |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| hours | — | < .001 | < .001 |
| anxiety | < .001 | — | < .001 |
| score | < .001 | < .001 | — |

The diagonal of the coefficient matrix is 1 because a column correlates perfectly with itself. The diagonal of the p-value matrix is an em dash for that same reason. A positive coefficient means the columns rise together. A negative one means one falls as the other rises. r near 0.98 is a very tight line in this toy set.

## Coming from SPSS

Analyze → Correlate → Bivariate.

SPSS puts Pearson, Spearman, and Kendall in one dialog, which matches this page. SPSS can also print a scatterplot from Chart Builder. Tensr’s chart switch is the shared `include_charts` option.
