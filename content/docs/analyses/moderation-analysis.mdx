title: Moderation
description: Test whether one predictor’s slope depends on a second predictor.

## When to use it

Use moderation when you think the slope of X changes across levels of M. For example: does an extra study hour buy more exam points when anxiety is low than when anxiety is high? The test is the product term, hours × anxiety.

## Assumptions

Same straight-line model as [linear regression](/docs/analyses/linear-regression), plus the product term. Tensr centers the predictors by default before building that product, so the hours slope is the slope at average anxiety.

## Running it in Tensr

<Steps>
<Step>Analyze → Regression → Moderation. In chat: “Does anxiety moderate the effect of hours on score?”</Step>
<Step>Outcome, predictor, and moderator are three numeric columns.</Step>
<Step>Center variables starts on. Confidence level starts at 0.95.</Step>
</Steps>

## Reading the output

Same 24 students. Outcome `score`, predictor `hours`, moderator `anxiety`. Centering on.

| Term | B | SE | p |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Intercept | 73.892 | 0.317 | < .001 |
| hours | 3.315 | 0.328 | < .001 |
| anxiety | -2.047 | 0.295 | < .001 |
| hours × anxiety | 0.124 | 0.064 | .065 |

The interaction p is .065, which is not below .05. The summary says the interaction is not statistically significant at the 95% level, so moderation is not supported. R² is 0.99, adjusted R² is 0.988, model F is 656.483, and the model p is < .001. The model as a whole fits. The product term does not clear .05.

Simple slopes of hours:

| Moderator level | Slope |
| --- | ---: |
| Low M (−1 SD) | 3.054 |
| Mean M | 3.315 |
| High M (+1 SD) | 3.575 |

These are the hours slopes at low, average, and high anxiety. They are close together, which matches an interaction that did not reach .05.

## Reporting (APA 7)

Exam score was regressed on study hours, anxiety, and their product (N = 24), with hours and anxiety centered. The interaction was not significant, B = 0.12, SE = 0.06, p = .065. The hours slope at average anxiety was 3.32, p < .001.

## Coming from SPSS

Analyze → Regression → Moderation.

SPSS does not ship a single Moderation dialog in the base menus. The usual path is Linear Regression with a computed product term. Tensr builds that product and the simple slopes.
