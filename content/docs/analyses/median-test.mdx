## When to use it

Use the median test when you want a simple check of whether groups differ in the chance of landing above the combined median. Kruskal–Wallis uses the full ranking and is usually more informative.

## Assumptions

At least two groups, each with enough scores to compare. The outcome is numeric.

## Running it in Tensr

<Steps>
<Step>Analyze → Compare Means → Median Test. In chat: “Median test of score by method.”</Step>
<Step>Group column and value column are required.</Step>
</Steps>
