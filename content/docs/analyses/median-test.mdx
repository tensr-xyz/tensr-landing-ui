title: Median test
description: Compare groups by how many scores sit above a shared median.
category: Non-parametric
---

## When to use it

Use the median test when you want a simple check of whether groups differ in the chance of landing above the combined median. Kruskal–Wallis uses the full ranking and is usually more informative.

## Assumptions

At least two groups, each with enough scores to compare. The outcome is numeric.

## Running it in Tensr

<Steps>
<Step>Analyze → Compare Means → Median Test. In chat: “Median test of score by method.”</Step>
<Step>Group column and value column are required.</Step>
</Steps>

## Reading the output

Six lecture, six online, and six workshop scores. The grand median was 73.5.

| χ² | p | Grand median |
| ---: | --- | ---: |
| 12.000 | .002 | 73.5 |

p = .002, so the groups do not split the same way around that median. The result also stores the above/below table.

## Reporting (APA 7)

A median test showed that the groups differed around the grand median of 73.5, χ²(2, N = 18) = 12.00, p = .002.
