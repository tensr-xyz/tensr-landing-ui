## When to use it

Use the Wilcoxon signed-rank test instead of a [paired t-test](/docs/analyses/ttest-paired) when the differences are skewed or the scores are ordinal. It ranks the size of each person’s change and looks at the direction.

## Assumptions

Each row is one pair. Differences of zero are ties and drop out of the rank sum.

## Running it in Tensr

<Steps>
<Step>Analyze → Nonparametric Tests → 2 Related Samples. In chat: “Wilcoxon signed-rank of before and after.”</Step>
<Step>Before column and after column are the two measurements. The ranked difference is before minus after.</Step>
</Steps>

## Reading the output

The before and after course scores. Most people gain, some lose a little, so the signed-rank statistic is not zero.
