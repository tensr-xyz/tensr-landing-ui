title: Moses test of extreme reactions
description: Compare how spread out two groups are after trimming extremes.
category: Non-parametric
---

## When to use it

Use the Moses test when you care about the range of scores in two groups, not the typical score. It trims a fraction of each group’s extremes and then compares the spans.

## Assumptions

Exactly two groups. Each group needs at least two scores. Trim fraction starts at 0.05 and must sit between 0 and 0.25.

## Running it in Tensr

<Steps>
<Step>Analyze → Nonparametric Tests → Moses. In chat: “Moses test of score by method.”</Step>
<Step>Group column must have two levels. Value column is the score.</Step>
</Steps>
