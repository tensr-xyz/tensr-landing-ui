---
title: Moses test of extreme reactions
description: Compare how spread out two groups are after trimming extremes.
category: Non-parametric
---

## When to use it

Use the Moses test when you care about the range of scores in two groups, not the typical score. It trims a fraction of each group’s extremes and then compares the spans.

## Assumptions

Exactly two groups. Each group needs at least two scores. Trim fraction starts at 0.05 and must sit between 0 and 0.25.

## Running it in Tensr

<Steps>
<Step>Analyze → Nonparametric Tests → Moses. In chat: “Moses test of score by method.”</Step>
<Step>Group column must have two levels. Value column is the score.</Step>
</Steps>

## Reading the output

The same two similar tutorial groups, asking whether one group has more extreme scores than the other.

Moses test of extreme reaction for `score` by `group`. Metrics: S statistic = 70; p-value = .500; Range ratio = 1.29; Range group 1 = 37.8; Range group 2 = 29.3.

The span after trimming is 70 and p = .500. The two groups do not differ in extreme reactions. That is the expected result for two samples drawn from the same spread.

## Reporting (APA 7)

A Moses test of extreme reactions comparing score in groups A and B was not significant, S = 70, p = .500. The groups did not differ in spread.

## Coming from SPSS

Analyze → Nonparametric Tests → Moses. SPSS prints a significance for the span. Tensr reports the span S, the p-value, and the two ranges.
