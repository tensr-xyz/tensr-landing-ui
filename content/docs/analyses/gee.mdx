## When to use it

Use GEE when the outcome is yes or no and the rows cluster, and you want the average effect across clusters. Pass or fail inside a clinic is the usual case. A random intercept you want to read as a variance is a [GLMM](/docs/analyses/generalized-linear-mixed-model).

## Assumptions

The outcome is binary. The predictors are numeric. The grouping column has at least two clusters. The working correlation is exchangeable: two people in the same cluster are treated as equally correlated. There is no switch for the family or the correlation.

## Running it in Tensr

<Steps>
<Step>Multivariate → Mixed Models → GEE (clustered binary). In chat: “GEE of passed on hours, clustered by clinic.”</Step>
<Step>The outcome is the binary column. Predictors need at least one numeric column. Grouping variable is the cluster.</Step>
</Steps>

The dialog reuses the mixed-model form, so it also shows random slopes and a REML switch, and it labels the dependent as numeric. This request sends the outcome, the predictors, and the group. The outcome has to be binary. Random slopes and REML are not part of this fit.

## Reading the output

Same 96 rows as the [linear mixed model](/docs/analyses/linear-mixed-model) page: twelve clinics, eight people each. The outcome is `passed` (58 of 96). The predictor is `hours`.

| Term | Coefficient | SE | z | p | 95% CI low | 95% CI high |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| const | -0.92 | 0.421 | -2.182 | .029 | -1.746 | -0.094 |
| hours | 0.333 | 0.085 | 3.926 | < .001 | 0.167 | 0.499 |

The hours coefficient is on the log-odds scale. The interval excludes 0, and p < .001, so more hours go with higher odds of passing in this set. The standard errors are cluster-robust. Metrics are Family binomial, Correlation exchangeable, 12 groups, and 96 observations.

## Reporting (APA 7)

A GEE model with an exchangeable working correlation predicted passing from study hours, clustered by clinic (N = 96, 12 clinics). Each extra hour was associated with higher log-odds of passing, coefficient = 0.33, 95% CI [0.17, 0.50], p < .001.

## Coming from SPSS

The product menu is Multivariate → Mixed Models → GEE (clustered binary). SPSS fits this with GENLIN, a binomial distribution, and a repeated subject with an exchangeable correlation. The path string stored for this item says Analyze → Generalized Linear Models → GEE. The item itself is on the Multivariate menu.
