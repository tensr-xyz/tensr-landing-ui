## When to use it

Use Kruskal–Wallis instead of a [one-way ANOVA](/docs/analyses/anova-oneway) when you have three or more groups and the scores are ordinal or skewed.

## Assumptions

Groups are independent. The outcome is at least ordinal. The test does not tell you which groups differ until you turn on post hoc.

## Running it in Tensr

<Steps>
<Step>Analyze → Nonparametric Tests → K Independent Samples. In chat: “Kruskal–Wallis of score by method.”</Step>
<Step>Group column and value column are required. Post hoc starts off.</Step>
</Steps>
