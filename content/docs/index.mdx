Take a study from the file you uploaded to the banner book you send, with every number traced back to the people who gave it.

The pages below are the parts of that job you repeat:

- [Import](/docs/import): bring the survey file in, labels and all
- [Merging](/docs/merging): join or stack two files, or fuse three or more
- [Levelled data](/docs/levelled-data): table a loop, such as brands per respondent, at that level
- [Weights](/docs/weights): rake a new version, and keep the file you uploaded
- [The agent](/docs/agent): Ask, Plan, and Agent
- [Significance and provenance](/docs/significance): letters, the low-base skip, and click-through to the people behind a cell
- [Export](/docs/export): take the book out to Excel or PowerPoint, and the file out with its weight syntax
- [The R script](/docs/r-script): the script for a run, and what the badges mean
- [Sharing and comments](/docs/sharing): a session link, and comments on a report
- [Using plugins](/docs/using-plugins): install from the marketplace
- [Building a plugin](/docs/building-a-plugin): publish a zip and, if you charge, get paid in pounds

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Agencies comparing Tensr with Displayr or Q on a live job. The documented analyses are the pages in this sidebar.
</Callout>
