Tensr is a statistical workspace for mid-size research agencies. You upload a survey file, build a weighted banner, and export the book your client already expects — Excel and PowerPoint, with significance letters and a path back to the rows that produced them.

These docs cover the parts of that job you repeat on every study:

- Importing `.sav`, `.dta`, and CSV
- Rim weights and nested banners
- Significance letters, low-base flags, and provenance
- Exporting the tables you signed off in the grid

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Agencies comparing Tensr with Displayr or Q on a live job. If you need a procedure menu of eighty tests, this is the wrong tool.
</Callout>
