Tensr is a statistical workspace for mid-size research agencies. You upload a survey file, build a weighted banner, and export the book your client already expects — Excel and PowerPoint, with significance letters and a path back to the rows that produced them.

These docs cover the parts of that job you repeat on every study:

- [Import](/docs/import) — `.sav`, `.dta`, CSV, Excel, Triple-S, Parquet
- [Merging](/docs/merging) — join or stack two files; fuse for three or more
- [Weights](/docs/weights) — raking a new version, not SPSS Weight Cases
- [The agent](/docs/agent) — Ask, Plan, and Agent
- [Significance and provenance](/docs/significance) — letters, low-base skip, click-through
- [Export](/docs/export) — Excel, `.sav` with the `.sps` weight file, PowerPoint
- [The R script](/docs/r-script) — what is emitted and what the badges mean

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Agencies comparing Tensr with Displayr or Q on a live job. If you need a procedure menu of eighty tests, this is the wrong tool.
</Callout>
