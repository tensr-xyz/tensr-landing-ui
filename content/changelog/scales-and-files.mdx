title: Scales and mixed columns
date: 2026-10-15
version: 0.4.6
tags: [fix]
---

A 1 to 5, 1 to 7, or 1 to 10 scale shows whole numbers and offers Top 2 box and Bottom 2 box. A nominal variable such as gender does not. A column that mixes text and numbers is saved as text, with a warning, instead of failing the upload.

- Suggested questions skip id columns such as RespID
- Opening a report uses the saved run when the tab is gone
- If that dataset was deleted, the message says so
