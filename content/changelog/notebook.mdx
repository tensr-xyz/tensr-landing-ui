R cells load the open dataset on the server, with the column types from the file. A long sheet no longer gets pasted into the cell, and a failed cell shows the last line of the error.

- Shift+Enter and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter run the cell
- Internal columns such as _row_uid stay out of df
- The full traceback is under Details
