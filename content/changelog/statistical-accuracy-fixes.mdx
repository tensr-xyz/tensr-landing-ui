title: Statistical accuracy fixes, checked against R
date: 2026-09-28
version: 0.4.1
tags: [fix]
---

We compared Tensr's output against R across the core analyses and fixed every mismatch we found. Each fix now has a test that checks it against R on the same data.

- Tukey post-hoc tables fill in standard errors and show very small p-values as < .001 instead of 0
- Mixed ANOVA between-subjects sums of squares now match R and SPSS, and Mauchly's test is hidden when there are only two levels
- Descriptives show the statistics you select
- Negative binomial regression estimates dispersion rather than fixing it at 1, so standard errors and p-values are correct
- Cohen's kappa is correct (it could previously show 1), and weighted kappa now reports SE and p
- Lilliefors p-values match R's nortest
- The Moses test runs again and reports a p-value
- Effect-size labels use one rule at cutoffs and cite their source
- Written summaries only mention your own variables
