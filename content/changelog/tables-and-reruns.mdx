A banner net stays on that table. Saving a net onto the variable is a separate step. A 1 to 5 scale stored as decimals counts the same codes you see in the builder, including Top 2 box. Re-running a table from an Excel file keeps the R check, and a comparison in the summary has to match the cells.

- Q1 by Group with Top 2 box counts the people on codes 1 to 5
- Re-run of an xlsx table stays verified
- A false "highest" sentence is dropped
- Inline tables in an answer render as tables
