date: 2026-10-15

A significance letter sits on the column with the higher percentage, or the higher mean. The letter still names the other column. Upper and lower case still follow how small the p value is. The table builder and the analysis forms use the same fields as the rest of the app.

- On a 1 to 5 satisfaction table, the letter for the higher group sits on that group
- Suggested questions use a category for a crosstab, or they say to bin a continuous column such as Age
